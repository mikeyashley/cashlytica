How to Audit Bank Fees

A bank fee audit is a structured review of bank statements and analysis statements to identify overpayments, duplicate charges, and rates that drift above market.

The goal is simple: find a dollar amount the CFO can defend, then use that finding to drive a negotiation or a subscription decision.

What the audit checks

  • Wire transfer fees above benchmark
  • Account analysis and service charges
  • Duplicate monthly fees after account changes
  • Dormant services that were never shut off
  • Rate drift after a contract renewal

How Cashlytica helps

  • Groups fee lines and compares them to a benchmark baseline.
  • Surfaces the biggest dollar deltas first.
  • Turns the findings into a report the CFO can take to the bank.
