Treasury Management Software for Mid-Market Companies

Treasury management software helps finance teams manage cash positions, forecast liquidity, optimize idle cash, and control financial risk — without requiring a dedicated treasury department.

What does treasury management software do?

Treasury management software (TMS) centralizes visibility into cash across all bank accounts and entities, automates routine tasks like reconciliation and reporting, and helps finance teams make better decisions about where to hold cash, how to fund operations, and when to draw on credit facilities.

Enterprise TMS platforms (Kyriba, ION, FIS) are built for large corporations with dedicated treasury staff and six-figure implementation budgets. Mid-market companies — typically $10M to $500M in revenue — need the same visibility but without the overhead.

Core capabilities to look for

  • Cash position dashboard — a single view of today's available cash across all accounts, updated daily or intraday
  • 13-week cash forecast — a rolling short-term liquidity model that projects inflows and outflows by week
  • Bank feed aggregation — direct connections to your banking relationships so balances and transactions flow in automatically
  • Reconciliation — matching bank transactions to accounting records to close the gap between your books and your actual cash
  • Idle cash yield analysis — identifying cash sitting in low-yield accounts that could be earning more in money market funds, T-bills, or sweep accounts
  • Bank fee auditing — reviewing bank charges against benchmarks to identify overpayments
  • Reporting and board packs — structured outputs for CFO reporting, lender compliance, and board presentations

How mid-market needs differ from enterprise

Enterprise treasury teams have analysts who can manage complex TMS configurations and daily reconciliation workflows. Mid-market finance teams are often one or two people managing treasury alongside their other responsibilities.

The practical differences this creates:

  • Setup needs to take days, not months — no room for a lengthy implementation project
  • The system must work across multiple banks without forcing a single-bank story
  • Outputs need to be board-ready without manual formatting in PowerPoint
  • Pricing should match the scale of the problem, not assume a dedicated treasury staff

When to consider a treasury management platform

Signs a mid-market company should invest in treasury management software:

  • Cash position is tracked manually in spreadsheets across multiple bank accounts
  • The finance team can't answer "how much cash do we have available today?" within five minutes
  • Bank fees have never been reviewed or renegotiated
  • Cash is sitting in operating accounts earning near zero while alternatives yield 4–5%
  • Lenders or investors have requested a 13-week cash forecast
  • Month-end reconciliation takes more than two days
