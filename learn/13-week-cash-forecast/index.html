What Is a 13-Week Cash Forecast?

A 13-week forecast gives finance teams a weekly view of liquidity for the next quarter.

It is short enough to stay actionable and long enough to show when runway, timing, or payments will create pressure.

What belongs in the forecast

  • Opening and closing cash balance
  • Expected inflows from collections and receipts
  • Expected outflows such as payroll, AP, debt service, and tax payments
  • A minimum buffer line so risk is visible

Rolling vs static

  • Static forecasts are built once and updated manually.
  • Rolling forecasts move forward every week as the horizon advances.
  • Rolling forecasts are easier to keep honest and easier to compare to actuals.
