Idle Cash Drag

Idle cash drag is the annual yield that the company gives up by leaving excess cash in a lower-yield place than necessary.

The number is often more material than it first appears because it applies to the whole balance base.

Simple formula

  • Take the deployable excess balance.
  • Subtract the current yield from the available yield.
  • Multiply the spread by the excess balance to estimate annual drag.

Why it matters

  • The drag is a real cost even if the cash feels safe.
  • It can justify a treasury review or a new operating policy.
