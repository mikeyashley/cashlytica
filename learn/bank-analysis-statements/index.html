Bank Analysis Statements Explained

Bank analysis statements show how the bank calculated your monthly charges.

They are the source documents behind a fee audit and the best place to start if the bill feels too high.

What to look for

  • Fee code names that do not match the contract
  • Volume spikes that do not match actual usage
  • New monthly charges after a bank merger or account change
  • Analysis fees that changed without a clear reason

How to read it

  • Start with the fee code and description.
  • Check the unit rate and the total charge.
  • Compare the volume to actual activity.
  • Flag anything that looks duplicated or drifted.

Turn statements into findings

The statement should become a concrete finding the CFO can act on.
