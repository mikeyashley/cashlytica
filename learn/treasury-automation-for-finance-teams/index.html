Treasury Automation for Finance Teams

Treasury automation should remove repetitive work and improve the quality of the decisions, not hide the details from the finance team.

The best automation makes the workflow faster while leaving a clear audit trail.

Good candidates for automation

  • Forecast updates
  • Reconciliation matching
  • Briefing generation
  • Alerting on key changes
  • Fee comparison and benchmarking

What still needs human judgment

  • Policy decisions
  • Negotiations with banks
  • Risk tolerance
  • Approval of execution
