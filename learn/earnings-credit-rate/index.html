Earnings Credit Rate Explained

The earnings credit rate is the rate a bank uses to offset analysis fees on compensating balances.

If the rate is weak or the balance is not used efficiently, the company may be subsidizing the bank rather than the other way around.

What ECR does

  • It creates a credit against certain bank charges.
  • It is not the same as a deposit yield.
  • It matters when your balances are large enough for fee offsets to be meaningful.
  • It can hide cost if the statement only shows a net number.

Why teams miss it

  • They only look at the total fee line.
  • They do not compare ECR to current market rates.
  • They do not test whether the balance is being used in the most efficient way.
  • The bank’s terminology obscures whether the offset is actually competitive.

What to review on a statement

  • The gross charges before credits.
  • The actual credit applied.
  • The effective rate compared to a benchmark.
