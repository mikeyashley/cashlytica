Common Business Bank Fees

Most companies do not overpay one dramatic fee. They overpay a set of small charges that accumulate quietly.

The best audit starts with the categories that show up most often and are easiest to benchmark.

Fee categories to check first

  • Wire transfer fees
  • Account analysis fees
  • Monthly service charges
  • ACH fees
  • Positive pay and remote deposit charges
  • Dormant or duplicate service fees

Signs of overpayment

  • A line item above benchmark
  • The same fee showing up across multiple accounts
  • A rate increase with no corresponding service change
  • A fee that should have been removed but still appears
