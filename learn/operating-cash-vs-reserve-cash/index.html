Operating Cash vs Reserve Cash

Operating cash is the money needed to run the business day to day.

Reserve cash is the money that can be held more deliberately for safety, policy, or yield.

Why the distinction matters

  • Operating cash needs to stay liquid.
  • Reserve cash can often be managed more aggressively.
  • Treating both buckets the same is usually inefficient.

Policy questions

  • How much is the buffer?
  • What triggers a move from operating to reserve?
  • Who owns the review cadence?

Separate the buckets

Make the operating and reserve buckets explicit.
