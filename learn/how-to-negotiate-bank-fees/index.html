How to Negotiate Bank Fees

Negotiation works best when the company has evidence, not just frustration.

Cashlytica should help buyers gather the line items, benchmark them, and ask for the specific adjustment that follows from the numbers.

Before you call the bank

  • Collect analysis statements for several months.
  • Identify the fee codes that are above benchmark.
  • Sort the findings by dollar impact.
  • Decide whether you want a credit, a rate reset, or a service change.

What to say

  • Lead with the dollar impact.
  • Be specific about the line item.
  • Ask whether the rate can be reduced or credited.
  • Document the outcome so it can be tracked next month.

Prepare the ask

Use the numbers to ask for a fee reduction or credit.
