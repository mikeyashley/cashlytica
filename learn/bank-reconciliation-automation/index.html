Bank Reconciliation Automation

Reconciliation is one of the clearest places where a small finance team can save time and reduce errors.

Automation should match obvious items quickly and leave the edge cases visible for review.

What to automate

  • Matching known transactions
  • Highlighting unmatched items
  • Flagging anomalies or missing records
  • Creating a review queue for exceptions

Why it matters

  • Less manual work.
  • Fewer missed items.
  • Cleaner cash data for forecasting and reporting.
