Cash Forecast Accuracy

A forecast is only useful if the team can tell whether it is getting closer to reality.

Accuracy should be measured against actuals in a simple weekly comparison, not hidden in vague confidence language.

How to measure accuracy

  • Compare predicted cash to actual cash.
  • Track absolute error and percentage error.
  • Review the trend over multiple weeks instead of one-off misses.

How teams improve it

  • Reduce assumptions that are not grounded in data.
  • Update the forecast on a regular cadence.
  • Look for repeated timing misses by category.
