Idle Cash

Idle cash is cash sitting in operating or reserve accounts that could be earning more without compromising liquidity.

The real question is not whether the cash is safe; it is whether the cash is working hard enough for the business.

What to check

  • Whether balances exceed the operating buffer.
  • Whether the current yield is below available alternatives.
  • Whether the company has cash that could be segmented more intelligently.

Why it matters

  • Idle cash drag is a quiet but real cost.
  • It can compound across a large balance base very quickly.
  • A small yield gap becomes a material annual number.
