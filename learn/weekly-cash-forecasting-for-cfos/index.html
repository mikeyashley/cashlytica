Weekly Cash Forecasting for CFOs

The weekly cadence is the part that makes the forecast real.

A forecast that is updated once and ignored is not a treasury tool; it is a spreadsheet artifact.

Weekly routine

  • Review last week’s actuals.
  • Update inflows and outflows.
  • Check the cash buffer line.
  • Flag any week that needs action.
  • Summarize the change for the CFO or finance lead.

What the CFO should see

  • One clear risk signal.
  • One clear opportunity signal.
  • A simple note on what changed since last week.
