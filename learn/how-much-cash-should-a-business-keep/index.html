How Much Cash Should a Business Keep?

There is no single right answer, but there is a right process.

The amount of cash a business keeps should reflect payroll timing, supplier risk, debt obligations, growth plans, and the company’s tolerance for volatility.

Factors to consider

  • Monthly burn or seasonal swings
  • Debt service timing
  • Supplier and payroll commitments
  • Minimum operating buffer
  • Board or lender expectations

Common mistake

  • Leaving too much cash in operating accounts because it is convenient.
  • Treating convenience as a treasury policy.
