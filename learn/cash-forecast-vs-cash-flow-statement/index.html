Cash Forecast vs Cash Flow Statement

A cash flow statement explains what already happened.

A cash forecast explains what is likely to happen next and where the team should pay attention.

Key difference

  • The forecast is forward-looking and decision-oriented.
  • The statement is historical and reporting-oriented.
  • The forecast should be updated weekly or daily; the statement is usually monthly or quarterly.

How to use both

  • Use the statement to reconcile history.
  • Use the forecast to steer the next 13 weeks.
  • Use Cashlytica to keep both grounded in the same source data.
