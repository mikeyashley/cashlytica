Common 13-Week Forecast Mistakes

Forecasts fail in predictable ways: too much detail, too little update discipline, and too many assumptions that never get tested against actuals.

A better forecast is usually simpler, more regular, and more honest about uncertainty.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Forecasting monthly instead of weekly.
  • Relying only on the GL.
  • Ignoring scenarios.
  • Skipping actual-versus-forecast variance review.
  • Letting the model drift without ownership.

How to fix them

  • Keep the model weekly.
  • Use bank and accounting data where possible.
  • Track a base case plus one downside case.
  • Review the forecast on a fixed cadence.
