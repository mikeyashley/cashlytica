Corporate Cash Yield Benchmarks

Benchmarks are most useful when they tell you whether your current behavior is meaningfully below what is available elsewhere.

The point is not to chase yield at all costs; the point is to know when the gap is worth action.

What to compare

  • Operating account yield
  • Reserve cash yield
  • Market options available to the company
  • Any policy constraints that limit movement

How to use benchmarks

  • Look for large spreads, not tiny differences.
  • Ask whether the yield is worth the operational complexity.
  • Review the benchmark whenever balances or policy change.
