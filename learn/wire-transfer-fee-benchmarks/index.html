Wire Transfer Fee Benchmarks

Wire fees are one of the easiest places to find leakage because the line items are simple and the range is measurable.

What matters is not a perfect universal benchmark, but whether your actual charge is high enough to justify a conversation or audit.

Typical range

  • Domestic wires are often in a modest per-transaction range.
  • A charge that is far above the local market should be flagged.
  • International wires and special rails can legitimately cost more, so compare like for like.

When to challenge the fee

  • The rate is above the published schedule or the contract.
  • Annual cost rises without a volume jump.
  • The same rate appears across all accounts, even where service differs.
