Money Market Funds vs Treasury Bills vs Sweep Accounts

When cash is idle, the question is how to earn more without creating unnecessary operational risk.

The right answer depends on liquidity needs, policy constraints, and how active the treasury function is.

How they differ

  • Money market funds are often used for operational surplus cash.
  • Treasury bills are often a fit for reserve cash.
  • Sweep accounts are convenient but often not the best yield.

How to choose

  • Keep operating cash liquid enough for the business.
  • Move reserve cash into the option that best fits policy and risk tolerance.
  • Review the decision when balances or policy change.
