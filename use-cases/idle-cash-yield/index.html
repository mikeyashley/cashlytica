Idle Cash Yield

Start here when the treasury question is about yield, operating buffers, and what cash should be moved versus left alone.

This use case matters when the balance looks healthy on paper, but the company is still leaving meaningful money on the table by parking excess cash too conservatively.

What this solves

  • Balance is sitting too safely.
  • Yield is below what the company could get elsewhere.
  • The buyer needs a clear recommendation.
  • Policy has to stay intact while the cash gets more useful.

What good looks like

  • A deployable balance is identified clearly.
  • The current yield is compared against realistic alternatives.
  • The recommendation explains why the move is safe.
  • The CFO can act without a long back-and-forth.

What to show the buyer

  • Yield benchmark guide
  • Sample recommendation
  • Policy-safe next step
