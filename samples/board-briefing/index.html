A synthetic board note covering cash position, forecast trend, the bank fee review and the recommended action. A good board note is concise, defensible, and ties each item to an action and a risk.

Executive summary

Cash position: liquidity is solid, but the current recommendation set still shows $3,500,000 in excess cash that can work harder.

Fee leakage: the sample fee review shows $720 in annual fee opportunities on $2,280 of annualized fees, driven by wire pricing (see the sample audit report).

Forecast: the first two weeks of the 13-week model move from $994,762.50 to $1,104,262.50 in the base case.

Board attention

1. Fee remediation

Negotiate wire pricing and request waivers where possible.

2. Idle cash

Sweep excess balances to a higher-yield treasury option.

3. Visibility

Keep the 13-week forecast visible for week-ahead planning.
