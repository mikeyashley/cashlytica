Security and data handling

What happens to your data, how uploads are treated, and what Cashlytica is not, in plain English, before you share anything sensitive.

Minimum necessary data

Share only the records needed for the issue you want solved: bank statements or exports for the fee review, balances and transactions for the forecast.

Customer isolation

Ask us for our tenant-isolation explanation before you share data, and we will walk through exactly who can see what. Email mike@richiest.com.

Product boundaries

Cashlytica is software, not a bank or a money transmitter. During the design partner program it analyzes and recommends and does not initiate payments. The product also includes payment staging and approvals and a separately enabled execution module; neither is part of the program, and neither replaces your policy or your approvals.

What to expect from uploads

  • Use redacted files where you can, keeping fee lines, balances, dates and descriptions visible.
  • Statements load as CSV, Excel, BAI2 or MT940 files; PDF statements are not supported.
  • Share only the records needed for the question you want answered.

What the product is not

  • Cashlytica is software, not a bank or money transmitter.
  • It does not replace your policy, your approvals or your treasury judgment.
  • It is a decision-support layer, not a custody layer.
