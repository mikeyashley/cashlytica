Trust

Security and data handling

Finance buyers should be able to see, in plain English, what happens to their data, how uploads are treated, and what Cashlytica is not before they share anything sensitive.

What buyers should verify

Minimum necessary data

The workflow should stay focused on the smallest set of records needed to find fee leakage, cash movement, or forecast issues.

Customer isolation

Buyers should expect clear tenant separation and a straightforward explanation of who can see what.

Product boundaries

The product analyzes and recommends. It is not a bank, not a money transmitter, and not a replacement for policy or approval.

What to expect from uploads

  • Use redacted files when possible.
  • Share only the records needed for the specific issue you want Cashlytica to solve.
  • Expect the workflow to stay narrow enough that a buyer can understand the data path before they commit.

What the product is not

  • Cashlytica is software, not a bank or money transmitter.
  • It does not replace customer policy, approvals, or treasury judgment.
  • It should be evaluated as a decision-support layer, not a custody layer.

Use the product safely

The trust story should give buyers enough clarity to proceed without guessing about the basics.
