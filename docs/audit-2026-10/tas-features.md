## Product in plain language

Cashlytica is cash planning and treasury workflow software for finance teams that manage one or more companies. It combines uploaded bank statements, connected bank feeds where available, and QuickBooks Online or Xero data to show cash position, forecast cash flow, identify bank fees and idle cash, and help teams review payments, exceptions, and decisions. Higher plans add more entities and banks, daily workflows, debt and covenant views, and report generation. Payment execution is a separately granted, policy-controlled capability; live provider operation has not been accepted for release.

## Pricing and code-enforced access

- **Free:** $0, no card. Fee teaser/public tools only; plan limits are zero entities, zero accounting and zero independent bank connections. `frontend/src/lib/subscriptionPlans.ts`; `backend/app/core/plan_limits.py`.
- **Fee Audit:** $99 one-time, not a tier. Purchased non-refunded audit or Analyst+ unlocks full report/PDF. Code creates a $99 charge, applies a one-time **$99 credit** on the first subscription invoice when eligible, and refunds a purchased audit when the report has no meaningful finding. `backend/app/services/billing/subscriptions.py`; `backend/app/api/reports.py`.
- **Analyst:** displayed **$199/month or $1,990/year**; self-serve. One entity and one QBO/Xero connection by base limit; no independent bank connections. 13-week/12-month forecast, scenarios, fee/idle-cash/yield analysis, weekly memo, chat and consolidated views if extra entities are bought. Extra entity **$59/month each**, up to 4 (5 total). `frontend/src/lib/subscriptionPlans.ts`; `frontend/src/lib/entitlements.json`; `backend/app/core/plan_limits.py`.
- **Manager:** displayed **$1,499/month or $14,990/year**; self-serve. Five entities and accounting connections, five bank institutions; 36-month outlook, bank feeds, bank/books comparison, payment operations, FX/concentration views and daily memo. Extra entity **$200/month each**, up to 10 (15 total); extra bank institution **$150/month each**. `backend/app/core/plan_limits.py`; `frontend/src/lib/entitlements.json`; `backend/app/services/billing/addons.py`.
- **Director:** displayed **$5,999/month**, sales-led; 25 entities plus quoted blocks of 25, unlimited accounting/bank connections; debt, covenants, intercompany advisory and board packs. `frontend/src/lib/subscriptionPlans.ts`; `backend/app/core/plan_limits.py`.
- **Execution:** separate, admin/sales-granted module; Manager may initiate payments and stablecoin transfers only; Director gains other EXECUTE capabilities. Every execution call still needs enabled, configured policy, with dual control where required. The PRD's approximate add-on prices ($400–500/month for Payments; ~$4,500/month for Full) are **UNVERIFIED as enforced prices**: no self-serve Stripe price for execution is configured in the mapping. `frontend/src/lib/entitlements.json`; `backend/app/core/capabilities.py`; `backend/app/api/admin_billing.py`; `docs/stripe-test-products.md`.
- **Trial:** checkout request defaults to **30 trial days** and permits 0–30; annual interval is default. Stripe price IDs and actual charge amounts come from environment/Stripe, while displayed dollar prices are frontend/PRD constants. A code path alone does not prove the configured live Stripe prices. `backend/app/api/schemas/subscription.py`; `backend/app/services/billing/subscriptions.py`; `backend/app/core/settings.py`.

## Must NOT claim in marketing

- “Live,” “production-ready,” or “provider-verified” payments, bank feeds, accounting sync, billing or end-to-end onboarding. The current release gate says **not ready for release**; controlled provider acceptance remains open.
- “Real-time cash” or “guaranteed intraday balances.” Bank/book observations have source-specific timestamps and may be stale or missing.
- “Direct bank APIs,” SWIFT, host-to-host, automated SFTP feeds, or universal bank coverage. Bank connectivity is primarily Plaid where supported; MX code exists but its customer path is unverified.
- NetSuite, Sage, Codat, or every accounting platform. Direct code supports QuickBooks Online and Xero only.
- Automatic accounting reconciliation, journal posting, partial payment allocation, or marking invoices paid in QBO/Xero. Bank/books matches require human confirmation and do not write back.
- “Moves your money” as a general plan feature. Movement is a separately granted, policy-controlled execution path; live rail readiness is unverified. Never say all Manager customers can pay from the app.
- Guaranteed forecast accuracy, savings, yield, financial return, or immediate/continuous advisor actions. The code supplies calculations and views; customer outcomes and cadence are not established by this audit.
- SOC 2 certified, audited, or compliant; end-to-end encryption of all data; fully functional SSO. The repo has controls and an SSO stub, not certification evidence or a completed SSO callback.
- Unlimited banks on Analyst/Manager, or unlimited entities on any tier. Entity and institution limits are enforced server-side.
- “Free 30-day trial” without checking actual Stripe configuration and offer terms. The request defaults to 30 days; live checkout behavior was not exercised.
