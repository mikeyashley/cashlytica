# Cashlytica Marketing Website PRD

Canonical product direction for the Cashlytica public marketing website.

## 1. Purpose

The Cashlytica website should be built as a traffic → proof → conversion engine.

Its job is not to describe every product surface. Its job is to attract finance buyers with concrete cash problems, give them useful proof or tools, and move them into a clear Cashlytica conversion path.

Primary commercial wedge:

- Run a $99 bank fee audit.

Secondary conversion paths:

- Check idle cash yield.
- View a sample report.
- Start free.
- Contact sales for higher-advisory or execution use cases.

Avoid referring to the product or website as TAS in public-facing copy. The buyer-facing brand is Cashlytica.

## 2. Current State

The current site has a useful foundation:

- Homepage at `/`.
- Learn pages:
  - `/learn/bank-fee-audit/`
  - `/learn/13-week-cash-forecast/`
  - `/learn/treasury-management-software/`
- `sitemap.xml`.
- `robots.txt`.
- Google Analytics installed with Measurement ID `G-W1L7GJDSHX`.

This is a start, but it is too small to drive meaningful organic traffic. The site needs more intent-matched pages, conversion tools, and proof surfaces.

## 3. Core Strategy

Use SEO to attract finance people searching for painful cash problems.

Use tools, templates, and sample outputs to get them to interact.

Use proof pages to build trust.

Use the $99 audit as the conversion wedge.

Use Analyst, Manager, Director, or Treasurer as the next operating level after Cashlytica proves there is money to recover or cash to optimize.

## 4. Homepage Requirements

The homepage should act as the main conversion hub.

### Primary homepage CTA

- `Run a $99 bank fee audit`

### Secondary CTAs

- `Check idle cash yield`
- `See sample report`
- `Start free`

Do not split attention across too many app routes. Avoid making the homepage feel like a product directory with links to every app page.

### Homepage section structure

Recommended structure:

1. Hero
   - Message: `You’re losing cash. Cashlytica shows you where.`
   - CTA: `Run a $99 bank fee audit`

2. Problem
   - Banks overcharge.
   - Idle cash under-earns.
   - Forecasts drift.

3. Example finding
   - Show a concrete sample, e.g. `$18,240 annual cash leakage found`.

4. How it works
   - Upload statements.
   - Cashlytica identifies fee leakage and idle cash.
   - You get a CFO-ready report.
   - Upgrade through Analyst, Manager, Director, or Treasurer for the capabilities the team needs.

5. Product ladder
   - Free
   - Analyst
   - Manager
   - Director
   - Treasurer

6. Sample outputs
   - Bank fee audit report.
   - 13-week forecast.
   - Idle cash recommendation.
   - Board-ready briefing.

7. Proof
   - Case studies.
   - Benchmark data.
   - Example savings.
   - Screenshots or sample outputs.

8. Final CTA
   - `Start with the $99 audit`.

### Homepage language rules

Use buyer-facing product language:

- Free
- Analyst
- Manager
- Director
- Treasurer

Avoid older or internal ladder language in primary marketing copy:

- Cash Intelligence
- Connected as a separate tier
- Optimize → Operate → Execute
- TAS

Connected can appear as a Manager capability when necessary, but not as a standalone buyer-facing rung.

## 7. Comparison Pages

Comparison pages should capture high-intent search traffic.

Create:

- `/compare/cashlytica-vs-kyriba/`
- `/compare/cashlytica-vs-trovata/`
- `/compare/cashlytica-vs-ramp/`
- `/compare/cashlytica-vs-mercury/`
- `/compare/cashlytica-vs-spreadsheets/`

Each comparison page should include:

- who each product is for
- where the competitor is strong
- where Cashlytica is different
- pricing and implementation contrast
- best-fit table
- CTA to audit, sample report, or demo depending on intent

Positioning examples:

### Cashlytica vs Kyriba

Kyriba is for enterprise treasury departments. Cashlytica is for mid-market finance teams that need fee leakage detection, idle cash analysis, forecasting, and advisor-style output without a six-figure implementation.

### Cashlytica vs Trovata

Trovata is a strong connected cash dashboard. Cashlytica should position as not just a dashboard, but an advisor that finds leakage, recommends action, and produces CFO-ready outputs.

### Cashlytica vs spreadsheets

This may be the highest-converting comparison page. Many mid-market teams are still using Excel, not Kyriba.

## 8. Proof Pages

Finance buyers need evidence before trust.

Build:

- `/samples/`
- `/case-studies/`
- `/security/`
- `/faq/`

### Case studies

Case studies can be anonymized at first.

Recommended format:

- company profile
- starting problem
- what Cashlytica reviewed
- finding
- outcome
- CTA

Examples of strong proof claims:

- found `$18,240` in annual savings
- reduced reconciliation time by a measurable amount
- built a 13-week forecast in minutes
- identified idle cash yield drag

### Security page

The security page should cover:

- data handling
- redacted uploads
- tenant isolation
- encryption
- bank feed posture
- regulatory posture
- Cashlytica is software, not a bank or money transmitter

### FAQ page

Questions to cover:

- What documents do I upload?
- Can I redact statements?
- Does Cashlytica move money?
- How does the $99 audit work?
- What happens if no savings are found?
- What is Analyst?
- Do I need live bank connections?
- How does the audit credit work?

## 12. Technical SEO Requirements

The site already has sitemap and robots. Add:

- canonical tags on every page
- breadcrumb schema on learn pages
- FAQ schema where relevant
- Article schema with `dateModified`
- SoftwareApplication schema on product pages
- unique Open Graph images
- sitemap entries for every new page
- internal links from homepage to top articles and tools
- internal links from articles to tools
- internal links from tools to audit/signup

Every page should have:

- unique title
- unique meta description
- one clear H1
- canonical URL
- crawlable internal links
- clear CTA

As the site grows beyond roughly 15–20 pages, add a sitemap generation script and a shared page pattern so metadata, header, footer, and CTA blocks do not drift.

## 13. Analytics and Funnel Tracking

Track traffic quality and conversion, not just visits.

### Traffic metrics

- organic sessions by page
- search queries by page
- impressions
- clicks
- CTR
- pages with high impressions and low CTR

### Engagement metrics

- CTA clicks
- calculator completions
- sample report views
- template downloads
- scroll depth on high-intent pages

### Conversion metrics

- audit starts
- register clicks
- billing clicks
- login/register split
- app.cashlytica.com handoff by source page

The key question is which content creates users, not just which content gets traffic.
