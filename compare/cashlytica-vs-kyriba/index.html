Cashlytica vs Kyriba: Which Treasury Workflow Fits?

These products address overlapping treasury needs at different levels of breadth. Kyriba describes a broad platform spanning cash management, liquidity planning, bank-account management, payments, and risk. Cashlytica centers on cash visibility, 13-week forecasting, bank-fee analysis, and decision support for finance teams.

This comparison uses each company’s public product information, reviewed October 21, 2026. It is a fit guide, not a claim that the products are interchangeable. Confirm current features, integrations, implementation scope, and commercial terms with each vendor before choosing.

Decision lens

Start with the workflows you must run: daily cash positioning, short-term forecasting, bank-account and payment controls, fee review, or risk management. Then compare the systems against your bank and ERP footprint, control requirements, implementation capacity, and total quoted cost.

What does Kyriba cost?

Kyriba does not publish a standard price on the product pages reviewed for this comparison; it asks prospects to request a demo and discuss requirements with sales. Avoid relying on an online estimate as a quote. Ask for a line-item view of modules, bank connectivity, implementation, support, contract term, and any transaction or entity charges.

Cashlytica is in pre-release and has no public price list yet; plans and pricing are set with design partners. That is not an apples-to-apples price for a full treasury-management platform. For either vendor, compare the annual cost of the workflows you will actually use. Our Kyriba pricing guide includes a quote checklist and cost model.

When each may fit

Consider Kyriba when

  • You need a broad treasury platform across multiple banks, entities, currencies, and payment or risk workflows.
  • Your team has the implementation, integration, and governance capacity to deploy the scope you need.
  • You want to evaluate an established platform against detailed bank, ERP, security, and control requirements.

Consider Cashlytica when

  • Your immediate problem is quantifying bank-fee leakage, reviewing operating cash, or building a practical short-term forecast.
  • You want to inspect sample outputs or apply to the design partner program before evaluating broader workflows.
  • You do not need the same breadth of payment execution and global treasury controls described by a full TMS.

These are starting hypotheses. Run both vendors through the same scenario: connect the same representative accounts, reproduce a cash position, explain a forecast variance, identify a fee issue, and document the people and systems needed to keep each workflow current.

Questions to ask in a treasury software evaluation

  1. Which of our banks, ERPs, legal entities, and currencies are supported today?
  2. What is included in implementation, and who owns mapping, testing, and ongoing maintenance?
  3. Can we trace each forecast input and cash balance to its source and timestamp?
  4. Which payment, approval, and segregation-of-duty controls are included?
  5. What is the full first-year and renewal cost for our selected modules and integrations?
  6. Can the vendor demonstrate our exact fee-analysis and forecast scenarios using sample data?
