Bank dashboards and spend platforms

A bank's own business dashboard, such as Mercury's, shows the accounts held at that bank. Cashlytica is built for companies with accounts at several banks, so it shows them side by side with book balances and a forecast.

A spend platform, such as Ramp, manages cards, bills and spend controls. Cashlytica does not manage spend; it covers cash position, forecasting and the bank fee review. Many companies use both. Descriptions of other products are general, so check each vendor's current materials.
