Cashlytica Compare

Cashlytica vs Trovata

Trovata is known for connected cash visibility through a dashboard. Cashlytica is a pre-release tool that combines bank, book and forecast data in a weekly cash review, with a bank fee review alongside.

Where Trovata is strong

  • Connected cash visibility
  • Dashboard-centered workflows
  • An established presence in the treasury category

Where Cashlytica differs

  • Bank fee review alongside forecasting.
  • Works from uploaded statements as well as connected feeds.
  • Pairs the forecast with a bank fee review and an idle cash check.
  • Still in pre-release, with design partners shaping what comes next.

Which to choose

Look at Trovata if you want a connected, dashboard-first view of cash from a mature product. Look at Cashlytica if you want to help shape a newer tool and need a bank fee review next to your forecast. Descriptions of other products are general, so check each vendor's current materials.
