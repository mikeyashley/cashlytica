Cashlytica vs Ramp

Ramp is strong for spend control and operational finance. Cashlytica is the better fit when the buyer wants cash position, fee leakage, idle cash, and treasury-style decision support.

Where Ramp is strong

  • Spend management
  • Controls around expenses
  • Operational finance workflows

Where Cashlytica differs

  • Treasury-native outputs
  • Forecast and yield focus
  • Bank fee and idle cash wedge
  • A more direct CFO/treasury lens

Choose the system for treasury work

If the goal is cash insight rather than spend control, Cashlytica is the better fit.
