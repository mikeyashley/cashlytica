Cashlytica vs Mercury

Mercury is a strong bank-native platform. Cashlytica should emphasize neutrality across the accounts the company already uses and the ability to see cash across the whole stack.

Where Mercury is strong

  • Bank-native experience
  • Modern SMB banking workflows
  • Integrated account management

Where Cashlytica differs

  • Neutral across banks
  • Treasury outputs instead of just banking surfaces
  • Audit and forecast wedge that works above the bank layer

Keep the treasury layer bank-neutral

If the buyer already has multiple accounts and wants a neutral view, Cashlytica should be easier to understand.
