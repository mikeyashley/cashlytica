Cashlytica for finance teams

Cashlytica brings bank, book and forecast data into one weekly cash review. Here is what each role tends to use it for.

CFOs

  • One weekly view of cash across banks and entities.
  • A forecast with scenarios you can defend to a board.
  • See the sample board briefing.

Controllers

  • Book balances from QuickBooks Online or Xero shown beside bank balances.
  • Possible payment matches proposed for you to confirm; nothing is posted back to your ledger.
  • Read more on bank reconciliation.

Finance teams

  • A weekly routine: update actuals, review the forecast, flag the weeks that need action.
  • Start from the 13-week template.

Founders

  • Runway visibility from a 13-week forecast with a minimum-cash line.
  • A first look at what your bank charges, with the bank fee calculator.

Mid-market companies

Teams with several entities and banks but no treasury department. See what to look for in treasury software.
