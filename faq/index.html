FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the design partner program, statements and data.

What documents do I upload?

Upload bank or analysis statements as CSV, Excel, BAI2 or MT940 files. PDF statements are not supported. Include any exports that help explain the fees, covering the period or account you want reviewed.

Can I redact statements?

Yes, but keep the fee lines, balances, dates, and transaction descriptors visible. If too much is redacted, the review may miss fee patterns or savings opportunities.

Does Cashlytica move money?

Cashlytica is software, not a bank or money transmitter. During the design partner program it analyzes and recommends and does not initiate payments. The product also includes tools to stage and approve payments, and a separately enabled execution module; payment execution is not part of the program.

How does the bank fee review work?

Design partners connect bank data or upload statements and get back a clear review of fee leakage and next steps. It is an automated review of your fee data, not a formal financial-statement audit.

What happens if no savings are found?

You still get a useful review of the files and a straightforward answer. If there is no meaningful savings opportunity, we will say so directly rather than stretching for a result.

What does it cost?

Plans and pricing are set with design partners before general release. Design partners pay nothing for up to six months and lock in half price on year one of Analyst or Manager.

Do I need live bank connections?

No. You can start from uploaded statements (CSV, Excel, BAI2 or MT940). Bank feeds connect through Plaid where your bank supports it, and are optional.

How do I join the design partner program?

Apply on the design partners page or email mike@richiest.com. Five places are available.
