FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions that usually slow a buyer down.

What documents do I upload?

Upload the bank statements or analysis statements you want reviewed, plus any exports that help explain the fees. If you have a specific question, send the file set that covers that period or account.

Can I redact statements?

Yes, but keep the fee lines, balances, dates, and transaction descriptors visible. If too much is redacted, the audit may miss fee patterns or savings opportunities.

Does Cashlytica move money?

No. Cashlytica is software and advisory support; it does not move money or act as a bank or money transmitter.

How does the $99 audit work?

You start with the audit, share the relevant files, and get back a clear review of fee leakage and next steps. It is designed as the lowest-friction way to see whether there is enough value to continue.

What happens if no savings are found?

You still get a useful review of the files and a straightforward answer. If there is no meaningful savings opportunity, we will say so directly rather than stretching for a result.

What is Advisor?

Advisor is the ongoing service tier for teams that want continued monitoring, better forecasting, and recommendations grounded in their actual data after the audit.

Do I need live bank connections?

No. The site is intentionally able to start from uploaded documents and file exports. Live connections can help later, but they are not required to begin.

How does the audit credit work?

If you move forward into Advisor, the audit can be credited forward as part of the commercial proposal. The exact treatment is confirmed before you pay for the next step.
